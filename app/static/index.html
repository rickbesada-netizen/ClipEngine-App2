    What to look for

      Post on

      Quality and speed

      Videos over 30 minutes (streams) only get their most promising parts transcribed, using YouTube's "most replayed" data and loud moments, so a 3-hour stream takes minutes rather than hours.

      Sign-in for downloads

      Only needed if YouTube asks you to prove you're not a bot, or for Instagram. Firefox works most reliably; close it first.

      Free local AI (optional)

      Install Ollama from ollama.com and run ollama pull llama3.2 to have a free local model rate each moment and write the hook title. Works without it.

      Disk space

      Finished clips are kept.
